# 📸 Screenshots Guide

Add your screenshots to this folder (`docs/screenshots/`) using the filenames below. Once added, they will automatically appear in the project [README.md](../../README.md) on GitHub!

---

### Recommended Screenshots & Filenames

| Filename | Dashboard Tab | What to Capture |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `dashboard.png` | **`📊 Dashboard`** | Top KPI cards (Monthly Spend, Total Spend, Visits, Savings, Avg Basket), Category Donut Chart, Store Spend Column Chart, and the **Monthly Spending Evolution Table**. |
| `price_compare.png` | **`🏷️ Price Compare`** | Interactive product selector, Cheapest Store & Most Expensive Store cards, sparkline price trend, and the **Store-by-Store Price Evolution Table** (showing same-store price changes over time). |
| `seasonal_trends.png` | **`📅 Seasonal & Price Trends`** | The **Month-by-Month Product Price Matrix** showing unit prices evolving across calendar months with color-coded price spikes, inflation, and seasonal patterns (e.g., Ramadan, holiday peaks). |
| `store_matrix.png` | **`🏬 Store Matrix`** | The cross-store side-by-side supermarket price comparison grid with heat-map color formatting. |
| `mobile_upload.png` | **Mobile Phone Flow** *(Optional)* | A screenshot or photo of uploading a receipt from the Google Drive app or home screen widget on iOS/Android. |

---

### 💡 Tips for Great Screenshots:
- **Resolution**: High-DPI / Retina screenshots look crisp. 1200px to 1920px width is ideal.
- **Format**: PNG or WebP recommended.
- **Privacy**: If your personal receipts have sensitive card numbers or personal info, blur them or use test receipts before taking screenshots.
- **Theme**: Ensure Google Sheets is at 100% zoom with gridlines visible for the cleanest look.
